The Cross-Bridge Cycle

Work through the five steps of cross-bridge cycling, explore the interactive figure, then test yourself by ordering the steps and matching them to the diagram.

Overview

Five steps, one repeating cycle

Each time a sarcomere contracts, every myosin head repeats this cycle many times per second. Calcium determines whether the cycle can proceed past Step 3; ATP availability determines whether it can proceed past Step 1.
